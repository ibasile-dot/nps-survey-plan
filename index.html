1. What we're solving

No structured feedback loop from organisers. No NPS baseline. No comparable data across clients. We need measurable data that feeds product decisions, retention strategy, and OKR tracking — and a monthly understanding of progress, not just a yearly one.

2. Three surveys

Based on feedback from Barry and Oriana (April 10 call + April 27 reply): merged the onboarding and pre-event surveys (for first-time clients these overlap), reframed the renewal survey as an annual review, and locked in the timing for one-event vs multi-event clients. Each survey: 5 questions, ~3 minutes. All built in Typeform, auto-connected to Google Sheets.

3. How it works

  1. PlanHat task triggers — Based on event date, PlanHat creates a task for the RM. RMs can adjust dates or postpone with a reason.
  2. RM sends Typeform link 7 days before the relevant call — Gives the organiser time to complete it independently.
  3. If not completed by the call — RM walks through the survey on screen in the first 5 minutes.
  4. Single-event clients (Annual Review only) — questions are folded into the debrief call rather than sent as a separate survey. The RM submits the responses on behalf of the client via Typeform after the call (see callout below).
  5. Data flows automatically — Typeform → Google Sheet Dashboard (NPS, CSAT, friction areas all auto-calculated).
RM-submitted entries — keeping the data consistent: When the RM fills in Typeform on behalf of the client (Annual Review for single-event clients, or any survey not completed by the client), use the URL with ?source=rm appended. This populates a hidden field that tags the entry as RM-recorded in the dashboard — clients filling in the form themselves never see it.
  • NPS and CSAT scores must come directly from the client. Ask verbally on the call ("on a scale of 0–10...") and capture the literal number — don't estimate.
  • Open-ended qualitative answers can be paraphrased. Fathom recording is the source of truth if there's any doubt.

Why RM-led: Clients are more honest with RMs than TAMs. Historical email surveys consistently failed. This approach uses the existing relationship and call schedule.

RM Strategy Call Playbook

A best-practice resource for strategy calls — not a script. Conversation prompts to draw from when the moment is right. These are different from the survey questions: the survey captures scores, these go deeper.

NPS in every monthly call: On every strategy call, we ask the NPS question: "How likely are you to recommend ExpoPlatform to another organiser?" (0-10). This gives us a monthly pulse across all clients — not just a yearly snapshot. It's critical for OKR 3 (CSAT target) and for Tanya's visibility into how the platform is tracking month-to-month. Fathom captures these conversations automatically, and once connected to PlanHat, sentiment and NPS data flow into client health scores without manual logging.

Two NPS instruments, two jobs — they don't replace each other:

  • Monthly verbal NPS (this call) = ongoing trend line, catches drift between events, monthly OKR view for Tanya
  • Survey NPS (Post-Event + Annual Review) = formal benchmark anchored to a structured customer moment, comparable across clients at the same lifecycle point
StageConversation prompts (pick 2-3)
Setup / Onboarding
  • Walk me through how you approached the setup — what did you tackle first?
  • When you got stuck, what did you do? Who did you go to?
  • What did you expect the platform to do that it didn't?
Pre-Event
  • What's your biggest worry about the event going live?
  • Tell me about the last time you had to contact support — what happened?
  • If you had an extra hour this week, what would you spend it on?
Post-Event
  • What moment during the event made you think "this works" or "this doesn't"?
  • What feedback did you get from your own attendees or exhibitors?
  • If you ran this event again tomorrow, what would you change on our side?
Annual / Relationship
  • What's the conversation internally about the platform — what do your colleagues say?
  • What's the one thing that would make this decision easy for you?
  • Is there a tool or workflow outside our platform that you wish was inside it?

After every call: Fathom → PlanHat (automatic)

Fathom captures every call. Once connected to PlanHat, sentiment, key topics, and NPS mentions are automatically extracted and linked to the client record — no manual logging needed. This creates the call-to-call continuity Barry asked for, without adding admin work.

Analysis & Tracking

Phase 1: April — Start collecting

Surveys go live now. RMs start sending them this month based on where clients are in the event cycle.

  • Typeform — Per-survey NPS score, response rates, score distributions, open-text responses. Built-in analytics, no setup needed.
  • Google Sheets — All 3 surveys auto-feed into one sheet. Dashboard tab calculates NPS, average CSAT, response counts. RM Links tab has pre-built URLs per client.
  • Fathom — Existing call recordings already capture NPS and sentiment data from every strategy call. This data will be connected to PlanHat in Phase 2.

Phase 2: May — PlanHat integration

Once PlanHat is set up, everything connects to client records for a full picture.

  • Survey scores appear on the client record alongside usage data and support tickets
  • PlanHat triggers survey tasks based on event dates — RMs see "Send Post-Event survey to [Client]" and can adjust or postpone
  • Fathom → PlanHat: Call sentiment, NPS mentions, and key topics automatically extracted from Fathom and linked to the client record — no manual logging
  • Client health score combines survey data + Fathom call sentiment + usage + support tickets
  • NPS and CSAT metrics feed into the OKR dashboard

Phase 3: June - August — Deeper insights

Connect survey feedback with actual platform usage to understand the full picture.

  • Link survey responses with Mixpanel/Posthog usage data — if a client rates setup as difficult, what are they actually doing in the admin panel? Where do they drop off?
  • Explore admin panel popup surveys for contextual micro-feedback (Taimoor's Phase 2 from the feedback epic)
  • First "Organiser Voice" report — summary of NPS trends, top friction areas, feature requests, and recommendations for Product and Ops
